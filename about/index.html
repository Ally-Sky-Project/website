The Ally Sky Project

The Ally Sky Project is an independent, multidisciplinary science fiction universe exploring the fragile boundary between human reality and technological transformation. Blending serialized fiction, dynamic visual art, original music, and interactive web architecture, the project constructs an expansive, multisensory world born from a single creative vision.

First Principles & The Beginner’s Lens

This universe is an open creative laboratory. It is built and documented through the perspective of a beginner encountering these disciplines in real time—discovering music theory, visual composition, and narrative design through the act of worldbuilding itself.

  • Deconstructive Study: Generative models produce intriguing artifacts, but the real work lies in breaking down why they work. We take the output apart—isolating audio stems, studying MIDI arrangements, analyzing geometric framing—to extract the fundamental physics and creative principles beneath the surface.
  • Learning in Public: Rather than presenting an illusion of effortless mastery, the journey is shared openly: the iterations, the technical breakdowns, and the discoveries made along the way, driven entirely by curiosity.
  • Layered Worldbuilding: Writing, acoustic performance, digital synthesis, and code are treated as connected layers of a single craft, each informing the next to build a coherent, living universe.

Technology as Instrument

What does authentic connection mean when algorithms learn to mirror our words and melodies? To explore that question honestly, the medium mirrors the message. Constructing a universe of this scale once required a large production studio; today, computational pipelines, generative audio models, and algorithmic visual engines serve as modern instruments in the hands of an independent creator.

The machine is a resonant chamber. The human is the composer setting the trajectory, the hand on the fretboard, and the ear that makes the final call.

Technology & Production Colophon

Built with human direction, deliberate study, and computational pipelines:

  • Narrative & Direction: Core scripts, character bibles, and storyboards written natively by the creator, using iterative model workflows for scenario exploration and dialogue stress-testing.
  • Original Score, Sound Design & Voices: Live acoustic instrumentation tracked on a Yamaha C40 nylon-string guitar; vocal takes and field voice memos captured via Audio-Technica AT2020, Focusrite Scarlett 2i2, and DJI Mic systems. Synthetic vocal layers and anomalous voice generation designed via ElevenLabs. Atmospheric stems synthesized with Suno v6, analyzed for arrangement theory, shaped with custom command-line DSP utilities, arranged in GarageBand, and mastered in Audacity 4.
  • Visual Art & Album Design: Narrative concept art, storyboard sequences, and album packaging generated via the Google Gemini Developer API and bespoke visual pipelines, directed by original sketches, photographic references, and framing parameters.
  • Engineering & Web Infrastructure: Interactive reader environments, procedural visual generation scripts, and site architecture built and maintained in Cursor AI.
